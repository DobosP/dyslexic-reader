# Privacy Policy — Dyslexic Reader

_Last updated: 2 July 2026_

Dyslexic Reader is a free, on-device reading app. This policy explains, in plain
language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

**The short version: Dyslexic Reader does not collect, transmit, or sell any
personal data. Everything you read stays on your device.**

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Dyslexic Reader has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising,
and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not operate any servers
that receive your data.

## Your documents and reading data stay on your device

- Documents you open (PDF, Word, text, or pasted text) are **read and processed
  entirely on your device.** Their text is never uploaded anywhere.
- Your **library, reading position, bookmarks, notes, and reading settings** are
  stored only in the app's private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app
  removes them.

## Network use

Dyslexic Reader works fully offline once set up. It uses the internet only in
these limited, content-free ways:

1. **One-time OCR model download.** The first time you import a *scanned* PDF
   (an image-only PDF with no text layer), the app downloads a ~20 MB
   text-recognition model so OCR can run offline thereafter. This download
   fetches the model only — **none of your documents or text are sent.**
2. **Text-to-speech voices.** Read-aloud uses your device's built-in
   text-to-speech engine. Depending on the engine and voice you have installed,
   that system component may itself use the network to fetch or stream voices.
   This is handled by your device's TTS engine (for example Google
   Speech Services), **not by Dyslexic Reader**, and is governed by that
   engine's own privacy policy. You can choose fully offline voices in your
   device's text-to-speech settings.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — used only for the two purposes above (OCR model download and,
  where applicable, the system TTS engine). The app sends no personal data over
  the network.
- **Opening files** — when you choose to open or share a document into the app,
  Android grants temporary access to that single file. The app does not browse
  or scan your device's storage on its own.

## Children's privacy

Dyslexic Reader is not specifically directed to children and is not enrolled in
Google Play's Families program. Because the app collects no personal data, it
does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Data sharing and sale

We do not share or sell any data, because we do not collect any.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices ever change, we will update this policy and the
"last updated" date above, and note the change in the app's release notes.
